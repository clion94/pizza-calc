⚡ Over-fermentation

Airy + sticky after 3 days = too much activity before the fridge.

⚡ Sticking

Flour your hands with bread flour before touching cold dough. Never wet hands. Dust the bench lightly too — semolina if you have it.

Ball shaping · your other fix

After mixing, divide dough and shape each ball like this:

01 Place dough on a bare, unfloured surface. You need friction.
02 Cup your hand over dough. Drag toward you lightly. The counter pulls the bottom skin tight. Rotate and repeat 8–10 times.
03 Surface should feel taut like a drum. Pinch seam hard underneath.
04 Place seam-down in a pint deli container — just barely bigger than the ball. Loose containers = flat, sticky dough.
Process timeline
Baking sheet protocol
Invert + preheat 45 min
Flip sheet upside down, place on bottom rack. Max oven temp. 45 full minutes — not 20, not 30.
Bottom rack → top rack
4–5 min on bottom to set the undercarriage, then slide to top rack until cheese bubbles and edges char slightly.
Semolina launch pad
Build pizza on a cutting board dusted with semolina. Acts like ball bearings. Shake gently before launching to confirm it slides freely.
Light sauce, strained tomatoes
3–4 tbsp max. Strain your crushed tomatoes through a sieve first — moisture is the enemy on a baking sheet.
